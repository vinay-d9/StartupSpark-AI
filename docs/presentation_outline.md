# StartupSpark AI Presentation Outline

## Slide 1: Title

StartupSpark AI — Startup Pitch and Business Idea Generator. Target users: student innovators and entrepreneurship clubs.

## Slide 2: Problem and users

Rough ideas often lack a clear customer, value proposition, and validation path. Show the three user scenarios from the project report.

## Slide 3: Product workflow

Show: five inputs -> validation -> project-specific prompt -> demo/Groq provider -> structured brief -> validation plan.

## Slide 4: MVP experience

Demonstrate the sample campus-food idea. Show the concept brief, value proposition, MVP features, risks, interview questions, pitch outline, and checklist.

## Slide 5: Responsible AI design

Explain the separation of user-provided inputs, assumptions, and unverified claims. Mention demo mode, provider errors, secret handling, and the prohibition on invented market or financial facts.

## Slide 6: Advanced extensions

Show the user-input-only alternative comparison, editable unit-economics worksheet, and two-week evidence plan. Emphasize that calculations use assumptions and experiments collect evidence rather than prove a business.

## Slide 7: Technical implementation and tests

Show the module structure and report the observed result: 15 automated tests passed on 2026-10-25. Mention the remaining manual provider and deployment checks.

## Slide 8: Limitations and next steps

Demo output is template-based, hosted provider output can be wrong, and no persistent storage or deployment URL exists yet. Next steps are browser screenshots, GitHub push, Community Cloud deployment, and live smoke testing.
